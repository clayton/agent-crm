The premise

Traditional CRMs repeat whatever the seller typed. Agent CRM helps your agents act—and challenges whether the forecast deserves belief.

What’s inside

The useful parts of a CRM.
The interface left out on purpose.

NEXT

Know what to do next

Get 3–5 explainable actions ranked across urgency, effort, pipeline stage, and revenue at risk.

RISK

Nothing falls through

Find unowned, unscheduled, stale, overdue, uncontactable, and incomplete pipeline records.

FORECAST

Forecast revenue

Track amount, close timing, next steps, probabilities, commit, best case, weighted forecast, and target coverage.

CRO

An evidence-backed review calls out weak coverage, unsupported commit, slipped dates, and convenient assumptions.

SDR

Prioritize enrichment, separate sourced facts from guesses, and prepare outreach without sending it.

Bootstrap, then re-check
